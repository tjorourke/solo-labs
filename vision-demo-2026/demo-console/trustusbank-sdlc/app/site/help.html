Common questions

How do I send more money each day?

Right now you can send up to € 5.000 a day online. Change it in online banking under Settings → Limits, then confirm in the TrustUs app.

When does my money arrive?

If you send it before 15:00 on a working day, it arrives the next working day.

How do I order a new card?

In online banking under Cards → Replace card. It arrives within five working days.

What is the TrustUs app?

Our app for logging in and confirming payments with your fingerprint, face or PIN. No codes by text message.

How do I spot a scam message?

TrustUsBank will never ask for your PIN or password, or ask you to confirm a payment, by email, text or phone call. If you are unsure, hang up and call us on the number on this page.
