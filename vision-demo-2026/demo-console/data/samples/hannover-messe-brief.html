Hannover Messe 2027: stand brief

Kernwerk returns to Hannover Messe with a stand in Hall 12 focused on the new EG-Series of energy-efficient industrial gearboxes. Everything in this brief is already public or approved for press and social media.

What we are showing

  • EG-Series helical gearboxes, up to 97.5% efficiency across the full load range
  • A live test rig comparing an EG-Series unit with our previous generation, with energy use shown on screen
  • Condition monitoring that predicts bearing wear from vibration data, available as a retrofit kit
  • Recycled-steel housings, cutting embodied carbon per unit by about a third
