Summary of the complaint

Anna Schmidt works rotating night shifts on gearbox line 3. Between June and August 2026 she worked 38 hours of night-shift overtime that were logged in the time system but not paid. She raised this with Jürgen Weber by email on 2 July and 30 July, and in person on 14 August. She received no written reply.

Two colleagues on the same line, Mehmet Yilmaz (KW-198230) and Laura Becker (KW-211407), report the same gap for July.

Why this file is restricted

Line 3 is inside the scope of the proposed closure of final assembly at Werk München by Q2 2027. The works council has not been informed of the closure, and the decision is not public before the results announcement on 6 November. Any summary of this case therefore carries both the personal data of the employees named above and an unannounced restructuring decision.

What the works council asks for

Back pay for the 38 hours at the night-shift rate, paid into the salary account above before the October payroll run. A written explanation of why the hours were not released. A review of the release step for the whole of line 3.
